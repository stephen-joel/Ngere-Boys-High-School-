Ngere High School
Who we are

A school with a distinctive identity.

Ngere Boys High School is located in East Ngere, Seme Sub-County, Kisumu County, Kenya. The institution is also represented in current education contexts as Ngere Senior School.

The school community combines academic learning with discipline, leadership, participation and personal development.

Our identity

Knowledge is Power

The school's crest carries the words “Knowledge is Power”, alongside symbols representing learning, nature and growth.

The Crest

The school crest features the name NGERE, a pen, a tree, a branching path and an open book, set within a green and blue shield.
